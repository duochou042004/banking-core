# Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in Banking Core a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or any other protected characteristic.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, precise, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Protect private, security-sensitive, and regulated information.
- Avoid conflicts of interest or disclose them promptly.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, sexualized attention, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile until a community conduct team is appointed. The project may warn, restrict, suspend, or permanently exclude participants based on severity and pattern. Reports will be handled confidentially to the extent possible, with conflicts recused and retaliation prohibited.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The project will adopt named enforcement contacts as part of governance formation.
